16 /**
17 * @class Agent
18 * @brief The controller for one player, bridging the logic side and the operation side.
19 *
20 * An Agent is the unified player abstraction with two ports:
21 * - the logic side (@c LogicRunner on the server, @c Client on the client) drives the
22 * agent through the requestXxx / notifyXxx methods and listens to the replyXxx /
23 * spoken / operated signals;
24 * - the operation side (@c ServerConnection for the wire, or GUI / Bot for a local
25 * player) listens to the xxxRequested / xxxNotified signals and answers by calling
26 * the bare-verb methods (rockPaperScissors / actionOrder / action / upgrade) and
27 * speak / operate.
28 *
29 * Reply contract: the operation side must answer asynchronously (e.g. via
30 * QTimer::singleShot or after an event-loop round-trip), never synchronously from
31 * inside the xxxRequested handler. A synchronous reply re-enters the request handler
32 * and violates the designed async request / reply flow.
33 */
